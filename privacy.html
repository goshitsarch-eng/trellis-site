Trellis

Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026.

Local application data

Trellis stores your boards, cards, notes, files, preferences, and recovery data in its application-data directory on your device. No account is required for local use. Trellis does not send this content to a Trellis-operated server or include advertising or analytics telemetry.

Optional Google Drive synchronization

When you connect Google, Trellis requests basic account identity and email information to identify and display the connected account, and the Google Drive drive.appdata permission to read and write its own hidden application data. It does not request access to your other Drive documents.

Trellis uploads and downloads your application content, portable preferences, managed files, operation history, and deletion records to synchronize machines connected to the same account. Sync records contain device identifiers, timestamps, and signing certificates needed to verify and reconcile changes. This data is used to provide the synchronization features you choose.

Drive requests use HTTPS. Google stores the cloud copy under Google's standard service protections; Trellis does not apply end-to-end encryption to synchronized content. Google's handling of data is described in Google's privacy policy.

Credentials and permissions

Google access and refresh tokens stay on the machine that signs in. Trellis uses protected operating-system credential storage when available, including the Secret portal in Flatpak. If protected persistence is unavailable, sign-in is session-only. Credentials, device permissions, network configuration, and transient interface state are not synchronized.

Sharing and other connections

Trellis does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train machine-learning models. It shares synchronized data with Google to provide Drive sync and with your other signed-in Trellis machines. If you enable the separate LAN sync mode, content is shared with the devices you approve. Links or remote resources you choose to open may communicate with their respective providers.

Retention and your controls

You can pause synchronization or disconnect Google while retaining local data. Disconnect removes remembered Google tokens from that Trellis profile; it does not delete the cloud collection or local recovery history. You can also revoke Trellis's access in your Google Account.

Cloud history and deletion records are retained without automatic pruning in this version. To remove the cloud copy, use Google's controls for deleting an application's hidden Drive data. To remove a local copy and recovery data, remove the Trellis application-data directory on that machine after quitting the app. Deleting one copy does not remove copies on your other machines.

Google API Limited Use

Trellis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
