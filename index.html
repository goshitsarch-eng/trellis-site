Trellis desktop

Your boards, on your machines.

Trellis is a private desktop kanban app for macOS, Windows, native Linux, and Linux Flatpak. Organize boards, cards, notes, and files locally without an account.

Optional Google Drive sync keeps your collection and portable preferences available across your machines. Trellis connects directly to Google Drive and stores its sync files in your account's hidden application-data area. Each machine signs in separately. Changes save locally first and can sync after an offline session.

You can also use a separate LAN sync mode with devices you approve. Trellis does not operate a hosted sync server.
